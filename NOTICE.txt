  https://github.com/myshiqiqi/rime-wubi/tree/master
  https://github.com/iDvel/rime-ice

The public kit contains no upstream dictionary, decomposition mapping, upstream
Lua code, binary lunar database, OpenCC data, font, image, application binary or
Rime engine source. Users obtain both upstream repositories themselves.

The offline helper selects required resources from those local downloads. It
adapts Wubi dictionary header names/import identifiers so Wubi86, Wubi98 and
Wubi06 coexist. Original comments, attribution and dictionary entries remain.
Ice resources retain their contents; its default.yaml is copied under the name
ice_defaults.yaml so it can coexist with Wubi's default.yaml. New custom patches
route Ice to that defaults file. Source/output hashes and transformations are
recorded in the generated UPSTREAM-SOURCE.txt.

A locally generated installation ZIP contains third-party code/data. Those
materials retain their original rights and are not covered by this kit's CC0
dedication. No explicit license was found in the checked Wubi source snapshot.
Ice declares GPL-3.0-only and includes components from other sources under other
terms. Consult both projects and Ice's LICENSE and others/docs/Credits.md before
redistribution. The helper retains those two Ice source documents under
source-info/rime-ice/ in the assembled resources. User configuration changes and
header renames do not make third-party materials copyright-free or CC0.

Fcitx5 Android, its plugin, Rime and the existing keyboard chart retain their
existing licenses and rights. This kit changes none of their source code.
